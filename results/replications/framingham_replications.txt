Cause of the XGBoost difference (kaggle/xgb_env_check.py, same data, seed 42, xgboost 3.1.3):
  setting                          Windows laptop (12 threads)      Linux Kaggle (4 threads)
  no subsampling                   sum of margins -1652.539680      -1652.539680   (identical fit)
  subsample 0.8, all threads       -1667.324011                     -1669.426200
  subsample 0.8, one thread        -1667.651290                     -1669.426200
  subsample 0.8, four threads      -1678.506613                     (as above)
With row and column subsampling the fitted model depends on the platform and, on Windows, on
the number of threads. Without subsampling the fit is identical on both platforms.
Consequence: the Framingham XGBoost numbers of the main analysis are reproducible exactly only
on Windows with 12 threads; on another machine they reproduce statistically (bootstrap top-3
0.581 vs 0.580, seed top-3 0.890 vs 0.869), i.e. like another seed.
