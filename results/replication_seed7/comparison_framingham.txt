WHAT REPLICATES
 - Faithfulness, plausibility, and their margins over the null.
 - Bootstrap agreement is below seed agreement for every model, in both runs.
 - LR is the most stable under bootstrap; RF the least, in both runs.
 - Model swap changes explanations more than explainer swap.
 - Model class and training sample are the two largest sources; seed, background and
   explainer are the smallest.
 - SMOTE over-prediction and its effect on tree-model explanations.

WHAT DOES NOT REPLICATE NUMERICALLY
 - Test AUC and calibration slope differ between splits by more than their bootstrap CIs
   suggest (848 test patients, 129 events). Consistent with the earlier split audit.
 - The MLP: tuning picked 14 epochs instead of 62. The shorter-trained network is MORE
   stable under bootstrap (0.64-0.66 vs 0.53-0.59) and LESS stable across seeds
   (0.77-0.82 vs 0.85-0.90). The bootstrap-vs-seed gap for the MLP shrinks from about
   0.32 to about 0.14 but keeps its sign. Together with audit 4 (H3b) this says the MLP
   numbers depend on the training recipe.
 - Background-draw ICC is 0.91-0.94 rather than 0.95-0.97: still small, but not negligible.

Stage note: the Kaggle runner placed KernelSHAP last, so the plausibility and variance
report stages failed there for lack of kernel.npz. Both were re-run locally on the
downloaded replication outputs (no model refits involved) with XAI_SEED=7.
